How we match you with a vet Learn how your pet's needs are used to recommend suitable practices.

First you tell us about your pet, the care they need, your location, and any preferences such as appointment times or home visits. We then compare this information with participating veterinary practices and show the matches that best fit your requirements.

What happens after you choose a vet Find out how to contact a practice and arrange an appointment.

Once matches are found we send you a list of practices from which you make your selections. At this point, we will put you in touch with the main contact person to set an appointment directly with them. The veterinary practice will confirm availability, discuss your pet's needs, and explain any consultation fees.
